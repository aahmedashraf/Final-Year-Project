    Welcome to DII Inflammation Tracker

    This application helps you track your diet's inflammatory potential by logging your meals and calculating a daily score.

    1. Use the search bar to find food items you have consumed.
    2. Enter the quantity (in grams) and add the entry to your diary.
    3. Once you're done, finalize your daily log to see your overall Inflammation score and nutrient breakdown.
    4. You can also view the portion guide for help with serving sizes.
